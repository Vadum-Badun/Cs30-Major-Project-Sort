# CS30 Major Project

For my project, I decided to to continue building a game from my first assignment. Right now it's **a circle sorting cubes in right direction, left or right**. My goal is to make it look neat. It should have user interface with options for **choosing levels** for single player, **keeping results not just in local storage, but also as a "Leader's board"**, and **improve visual design**. I also have to work on game intself, **my focus will be on visual design and animations**.
